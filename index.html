Bikeshare plan: day pass + e-bike
  • Your bike legs: hotel → Peregrine (~5 mi, ~35 min on an e-bike), Capitol Hill → Union Market (~10 min), Union Market → Portrait Gallery (~10 min). Then Metro home.
  • Buy the day pass in the Capital Bikeshare app ($10). With e-bikes for all three legs (~55 min at $0.15/min) the day comes to about $18, vs. about $22 paying per ride.
  • If you Metro in instead and only bike the two short hops, skip the pass: pay per ride (a few dollars).
  • The ride in is easy on an e-bike: flat riverside trail most of the way, then a gentle climb up Capitol Hill at the end. You won’t arrive sweaty (mid-50s at 9am). On a classic bike it’s ~40–45 min, close to the 45-min limit, so dock once on the Mall to reset.
  • Check the app for an e-bike at the dock before you walk over; they aren’t at every station all the time.
  • Prices from capitalbikeshare.com, Nov 1.
Ride in: route, navigation, what to wear
  • Route (~5.5 mi, ~35–40 min):
    1. Crystal Dr north to the Water Park (look for the waterfall). The path on its south side tunnels under the tracks to the Mount Vernon Trail. Turn left (north).
    2. River trail past Gravelly Point, with planes very low overhead.
    3. New: a ~¼-mile Long Bridge construction detour north of Gravelly Point (there through 2030). Narrower, with concrete barriers, and it crosses a construction road. Slow down and follow the signs.
    4. Up onto the 14th St Bridge path. It’s narrow and morning commuters pass fast, so keep right. Monument straight ahead.
    5. Off at the Jefferson Memorial, up to the Mall, east on Madison Dr (paved; skip the gravel paths).
    6. Around the south side of the Capitol, then the protected lanes on Penn Ave SE up the Hill. Dock at the Eastern Market Metro plaza, a block from Peregrine.
  • Bike vs. Metro: about the same door to door (~35–40 min either way). The Metro is mostly underground. Take it only if it’s raining or you’re late.
  • App: buy the day pass in the Capital Bikeshare app. The Lyft app also unlocks the same bikes, but buy the pass in Capital Bikeshare.
  • No phone holder: I found no sign that DC bikes have them (NYC’s Citi Bike is testing mounts on ~10% of its e-bikes). Options: Google Maps bike directions with voice in one earbud; a strap-on silicone handlebar mount from home; or phone in pocket and check at the stops below. If you have a watch, cycling directions can buzz you at each turn.
  • Places to check your phone: Water Park tunnel → Gravelly Point detour → bridge ramp → Jefferson Memorial → Madison Dr → Penn Ave SE.
